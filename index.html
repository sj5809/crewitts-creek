About The Creek

Your Neighborhood Kitchen & Bar

Crewitts Creek Kitchen & Bar is Independence, Kentucky's go-to spot for real comfort food done right. With a full sports bar and a crew that treats you like family — come hungry, come thirsty.

Opened in June 2017 by two Independence neighbors, we've grown into one of Northern Kentucky's most-reviewed local restaurants. Known for our signature mac & cheese, hand-pattied Angus burgers, Yard Bird fried chicken, a full brunch menu and a bar stocked with Kentucky bourbon. Your home for UK Basketball.
